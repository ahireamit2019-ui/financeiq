Macro Economy

The big picture — growth, production and policy rates.

What These Numbers Mean

GDP (Gross Domestic Product)

The total value of all goods and services produced in India over a period. Faster GDP growth generally means more jobs, higher incomes, and stronger corporate earnings — which often supports stock markets.

IIP (Index of Industrial Production)

Measures month-on-month output from factories, mines, and power plants. Rising IIP signals healthy manufacturing demand and economic momentum; falling IIP can hint at slowing growth ahead.

RBI Repo Rate

The interest rate at which the RBI lends to banks. Higher repo rates make loans costlier (cooling inflation but slowing growth); lower rates make borrowing cheaper and can boost spending and markets.

PMI (Purchasing Managers' Index)

A survey-based score of business activity in manufacturing and services. Above 50 means the sector is expanding; below 50 means it's contracting — a quick pulse-check on the economy each month.

Popular Tax-Saving Options

  • Section 80C — up to ₹1,50,000/yr via PPF, ELSS mutual funds, life insurance premiums, EPF.
  • NPS (80CCD-1B) — additional ₹50,000/yr deduction for retirement savings.
  • Health Insurance (80D) — up to ₹25,000 (₹50,000 for senior citizens) for premiums.
  • Home Loan Interest (24b) — up to ₹2,00,000/yr on a self-occupied property.

Most 80C/80D deductions apply only under the old tax regime — compare both regimes before filing.
